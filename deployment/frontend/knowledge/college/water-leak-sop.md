# Water leak SOP

Generic anonymised example; replace with an approved local procedure before autonomous intervention.

## Verify flow

1. Check the affected meter against occupancy, pressure, and the recent baseline. Record the observed flow and affected building.

## Contain safely

2. Inspect the branch and isolate only a verified leaking section when safe. Keep essential water service available and route uncertain isolation to the head.

## Rain coordination

3. During heavy rain, coordinate with drainage and electrical teams before entering flooded areas or operating pumps.

## Verify repair

4. Compare readings after repair against the earlier baseline and record the resolution; estimates are not measured savings.
